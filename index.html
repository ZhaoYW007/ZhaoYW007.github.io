I an Assistant Professor at Indiana University Bloomington. I warmly welcome anyone interested in collaborating or discussing research with me, either remotely or in person.

I am seeking highly motivated students at all levels, including Ph.D., master's, and undergraduate, as well as for other research assistant opportunities. If you are interested in working with me, please email me your CV and indicate whether you are applying for a Ph.D. position or a research internship.

I design faster and more efficient computer systems and architecture. My research focuses on cross-stack co-design for modern and future hardware systems spanning three levels: (i) algorithms and data structures, (ii) systems and architectures, and (iii) hardware and circuits. My work aims to develop general and foundational techniques for designing future hardware systems that are both theoretically grounded and practically efficient.

I received my Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Phillip B. Gibbons in the Parallel Data Lab and the CMU Parlay Lab. Prior to that, I received my Bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University with highest honor in 2021. I also worked as a Research Scientist Intern in Meta in 2023 and 2025.
